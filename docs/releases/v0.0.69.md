## Highlights ⭐️

* **Inline stylesheets are minified**: the CSP pass moves each `<style>` block into `_csp/<hash>.css`, but it ran after minification, so those files shipped exactly as written, comments and all. They are now minified before they are hashed, and the SRI covers the minified bytes. One theme's homepage stylesheet went from 46.9 KB to 28.8 KB gzipped, and together with a smaller page it scored 1.0 for mobile performance in 5 of 7 local Lighthouse runs, up from 0.99 in every run.
* **Stylesheets and scripts keep their text**: the repair that escapes tags inside inline `<code>` spans also rewrote `<style>`, `<script>` and comments, so a stylesheet comment mentioning `<code>` turned the selector `pre>code` into `pre&gt;code` and the browser dropped the rule. Those regions are now left alone.
* **Highlighted code links a stylesheet that exists**: pages linked `/highlight.css`, but the build only wrote the fingerprinted `highlight.<hash>.css`. They now link the fingerprinted file, with SRI (#790).
* **Dependencies current and vetted**: ammonia 4.1.8, rustls-webpki 0.103.16 and webpki-roots 1.0.10 clear three advisories; staticdatagen 0.0.26 restores the news sitemap's XML declaration and brings comrak 0.56; every new crate version, including comrak's SIMD dependencies, has an audit in `supply-chain/audits.toml`.
